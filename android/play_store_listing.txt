--- FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters; paste into Play Console “Full description”) ---
Get Tip helps you remember every dollar when your income depends on tips.

Whether you run tables, pour drinks, or deliver orders, it is easy to lose track
of cash and card tips across shifts. Get Tip is a lightweight journal built
for fast entry after work: log the amount, pick the date, add a short note if
you want, and move on. Your entries stay on your phone so you can review totals
without juggling notebooks or spreadsheets.

See how you are doing over time. The weekly chart groups your tips by ISO week
so you can compare busy streaks with slower periods—useful when scheduling
extra shifts or planning expenses. The home screen shows a clear total so you
always know what you have recorded so far.

Need a clean file for taxes, a landlord, or your own budget? Export everything
to a CSV with Date, Amount, and Notes columns and save it to your Downloads
folder (where the device allows). No account is required for basic tracking.

Get Tip is designed for waiters, bartenders, delivery drivers, and similar
tipped roles: people who move fast, work irregular hours, and want numbers they
can trust at the end of the week.

Important: Get Tip is a personal log to supplement your own records. It
does not replace official pay stubs, employer reporting, or tax advice. Always
follow local laws and your employer’s policies for gratuities and reporting.

If you want simple, offline-first tip tracking with totals, weekly insight, and
CSV export, Get Tip is built for you.
